About Me

My name is Bruna Nascimento, I am from Rio de Janeiro and have lived in Porto, Portugal, for 12 years. I am a multimedia technician specialized in motion design, 3D and interactive digital experiences, dedicated to transforming ideas into memorable visual solutions.
I work from animations and motion graphics to innovative interactive digital experiences, always seeking to create unique and meaningful experiences.
This portfolio presents a selection of works that reflect my style and technique, showing how it is possible to combine creativity and functionality to create digital solutions that stand out and add real value for brands, companies and creative projects.

MOTION GRAPHICS, ANIMATION
Singularity
“Singularity” is an audiovisual animation created in After Effects that portrays the life cycle of a star, from its emergence to its final transformation. Using light, color and abstract forms, the piece guides the viewer through a cosmic journey that reveals ephemerality, energy and metamorphosis. An exercise in motion design and visual composition that symbolizes creative force and universal transformation.

Inside Lumon
“Inside Lumon” is a 3D animation inspired by the mysterious atmosphere of the series Severance, recreating the minimalist corporate environment of Lumon Industries. Created in Blender, with visual effects and post-production in After Effects, the project explores lighting, composition and movement to create a cinematic experience that reflects the series’ distinctive and enigmatic visual style.

A series of fictional animated posters created in After Effects, combining motion graphics and graphic design to bring the visual identity of the Beast film festival to life. 3D, WEBGL
Harmonic Hall
"Harmonic Hall" is an experimental WebGL environment where the inside of a cello becomes a stage and a navigable space. Originally created in Blender and imported into Unity, the project invites the user to explore the internal structures of the instrument as if it were an architectural hall. Inspired by the photographic series "Architecture in Music" , by Charles Brooks, the setting blends form, scale and sound to transform the musical universe into a visual space.

3D MODELLING, PROCEDURAL TEXTURING
Eye of Cthulhu
A 3D reinterpretation of the Eye of Cthulhu, the iconic boss from Terraria, reimagined through a darker and more unsettling visual aesthetic. Created in Blender, the project explores organic forms, procedural materials and shader development using nodes, combined with dramatic lighting and atmospheric rendering to bring this familiar game creature into a more cinematic horror setting.
