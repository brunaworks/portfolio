Singularity

“Singularity” is an animation created entirely in After Effects, depicting the life cycle of a star, from its birth to its final transformation into a black hole. The title refers both to singularity as “unity/originality” and to the “cosmic singularity/stellar collapse”, reinforcing the work’s symbolic ambiguity. The animation visualizes phases such as the primordial nebula, the star’s emergence, its expansion and finally its collapse into a dark point, representing energy, ephemerality and transformation.

"From stardust to singularity
Creation begins anew"

The phrase accompanying the work summarizes the animation’s central message. “Stardust” evokes the cosmic origin, the primordial material from which everything emerges; “singularity” refers to the final collapse, the extreme point of concentration of energy and meaning. This transition expresses the idea that each greater or more ephemeral cycle also offers a new opportunity for creation. In other words, matter, life and light, even after collapse, can give rise to something new, perhaps a new idea, a new form, a new perception.

Technically, the project combines layers, color transitions, light manipulation and multiple effects, creating its more stylized aesthetic. Without figurative graphics or literal narration, meaning emerges from light, color, movement and rhythm. “Singularity” functions as an exercise in motion design and visual composition, demonstrating technical control over digital tools and aesthetic sensitivity.
